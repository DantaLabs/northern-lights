# Demo Harbour Components Ltd (fictional)

An invented company for the Northern Lights demo. Any resemblance to a real company is accidental.

- Sector: manufacture of metal and plastic components for the marine and rail industries.
- Countries: Netherlands, Poland, Portugal.
- Reporting years: 2024 and 2025 (financial year equals calendar year).

## Sites

| Site | Country | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Demo Harbour Rotterdam | Netherlands | Head office and assembly |
| Demo Harbour Gdansk | Poland | Machining and foundry |
| Demo Harbour Porto | Portugal | Plastics moulding and logistics |

## Headcount

- 2024: 412 employees
- 2025: 428 employees

The 2025 figure equals the `employees_total` value in `data/esg-2025.csv`.
